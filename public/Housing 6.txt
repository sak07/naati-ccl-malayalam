Property inspection

Theerchayaayum. Veed kanuvanulla karyangal krameekarikkan njangalk  saadhikkum. Enn pokuvaanaan  thaankalk thaalparyam

Kshamikkanam sheniyaazhcha ravile saadhikkilla . Njaayarazhcha ucha kazhinj pattumo

Njangalk ucha thirinj 2 manikum 4 manikum njaayarazhcha slotukal labyamaayittund .  Ivayil samayamaan thaankalk  thiranjedukkan thaalparyam
