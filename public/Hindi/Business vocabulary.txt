Business Vocabulary - Hindi (व्यापार शब्दावली)

Business = व्यापार (vyaapaar)
Advertisement = विज्ञापन (vigyaapan)
Buy = खरीदना (khareedna)
Consumer = उपभोक्ता (upbhokta)
Price / Rate = कीमत / दर (keemat / dar)
Cost = लागत (laagat)
Afford = वहन करना / सामर्थ्य होना (vahan karna)
Consumer law = उपभोक्ता कानून (upbhokta kaanoon)
Refund = धनवापसी (dhanvaapsi)
Replace = बदलना (badalna)
Repair = मरम्मत (marammat)
Receipt = रसीद (raseed)
Manager = प्रबंधक (prabhandhak)
Bank loan = बैंक ऋण (bank rin)
Legal system = कानूनी व्यवस्था (kaanooni vyavastha)
Tax number = कर संख्या (kar sankhya)
Change = बदलाव (badlaav)
Charge = शुल्क (shulk)
Business owners = व्यापार मालिक (vyaapaar maalik)
Place = स्थान (sthaan)
Negotiate = बातचीत करना (baatcheet karna)
Price list = मूल्य सूची (mooly soochi)
Prospective buyer = संभावित खरीदार (sambhaavit khariidaar)
Commission = कमीशन (kamiishon)
Competition = प्रतिस्पर्धा (pratispardha)
Schedule = कार्यक्रम / अनुसूची (kaaryakram / anusoochi)
Meeting = बैठक (baithak)
Tax office = कर कार्यालय (kar kaaryaalay)
Colleague = सहकर्मी (sahkarmi)
Advisor = सलाहकार (salaahkaar)
Advise = सलाह देना (salaah dena)
Representative = प्रतिनिधि (pratinidhi)
Products = उत्पाद (utpaad)
Interested = रुचि रखना (ruchi rakhna)
Import = आयात (aayaat)
Export = निर्यात (niryaat)
Damaged goods = क्षतिग्रस्त माल (kshatigrasth maal)
Finished goods = तैयार माल (taiyaar maal)
Proposal = प्रस्ताव (prastaav)
Business plan = व्यापार योजना (vyaapaar yojnaa)
Supply = आपूर्ति (aapoort)
Demand = माँग (maang)
Popular = लोकप्रिय (lokpriy)
Successful = सफल (saphal)
News = समाचार (samaachaar)
Agreement = समझौता (samjhauta)
Invoice = चालान (chaalan)
Contract = अनुबंध (anubandh)
Profit = लाभ (laabh)
Loss = हानि (haani)
Revenue = राजस्व / आमदनी (raajasv / aamdani)
